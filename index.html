Ground Response

Relief Priorities on the Ground

Emergency interventions are focused on verified acute needs identified by local coordinators in impacted riverside villages and transit shelters.

Priority 1

Clean Drinking Water & Purification

Flooding contaminates community wells and springs. We distribute clean water jerrycans, filtration units, and water purification tablets to halt waterborne disease outbreaks.

  • Chlorine water purification tablets
  • 20L durable safe-water jerrycans
  • Community portable gravity filters
Priority 2

Dry Rations & Nutritional Packs

Immediate nutrition for families whose food stores and crops were washed away. We procure local staple dry grains, high-protein pulses, and ready-to-eat rations.

  • Rice sacks (10kg/20kg) and lentils
  • Cooking oil, iodized salt, and spices
  • High-energy biscuits and children's nutrition
Priority 3

First-Aid & Emergency Medical Kits

Immediate medical aid administered in coordination with community health workers to treat immersion injuries, wound infections, and diarrheal illnesses.

  • Oral Rehydration Salts (ORS) packets
  • Antiseptic wash, sterile gauze, and bandages
  • Essential fever and pain management medications
Priority 4

Temporary Shelter & Tarpaulins

Weatherproofing for displaced families living in temporary camps or damaged dwellings exposed to ongoing monsoon rainfall and cold nights.

  • Heavy-gauge waterproof tarpaulins & nylon ropes
  • Ground insulation mats and damp-proof sheets
  • Solar-powered emergency lamps
Priority 5

Clothing, Blankets & Essentials

Dignity and warmth for vulnerable elders and young children who lost household possessions to rapid flood currents.

  • Thick wool blankets and thermal wraps
  • Dry clothing packs for children and adults
  • Hygiene kits (soap, towels, sanitary supplies)

Accountability

Fund Transparency & Operating Model

We believe transparent custody and verified field deployment are the foundation of public trust. Here is exactly how funds are collected, protected, and spent.

1. Custody & Processing

Donations are received through a dedicated, official Razorpay merchant account managed by the organizer. Razorpay handles 100% of payment authentication and security. We never collect or store payment credentials.

2. Direct Supply Allocation

100% of net received donations (after unavoidable payment gateway transaction fees) are spent directly on relief materials, wholesale food procurement, medical supplies, and local transport to flood points.

3. Ground Logistics & Delivery

Relief packages are distributed directly by verified local volunteers and trusted community leaders who live in the affected river belts, ensuring supplies reach families cut off by washed-out access paths.

4. Public Verification Log

We publish dated procurement invoices, dispatch manifests, and photo documentation in the Dated Updates section below as each supply batch is handed over.

Zero Fabricated Metrics Policy: This site will never feature artificial real-time counters, simulated donor tickers, or unverified beneficiary counts. All distribution reports are confirmed by written receipts and on-site logs before publication.
