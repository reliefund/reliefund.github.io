Ground Response

Relief Priorities on the Ground

Directly funding essential survival goods procured locally and delivered to flood-affected river basin villages.

Water

Clean Drinking Water

Chlorine purification tablets, portable gravity filters, and 20L storage jerrycans to prevent waterborne disease.

Food

Dry Rations & Meals

Staple grains (10kg rice sacks, lentils, cooking oil, salt) and high-energy biscuits for displaced families.

Health

First Aid & Medicine

Oral rehydration salts (ORS), wound care antiseptics, sterile bandages, and basic fever medications.

Shelter

Shelter & Warmth

Heavy-duty waterproof tarpaulins, tie ropes, insulation ground sheets, and thick wool blankets.

Accountability

Fund Transparency & Operating Model

We believe transparent custody and verified field deployment are the foundation of public trust.

1. Secure Razorpay Custody

Donations route directly to our official Razorpay account. No banking or card credentials are ever seen or stored on this website.

2. 100% Net Direct Allocation

100% of net donations are used exclusively for wholesale purchasing of relief materials and local delivery. Zero overhead deductions.

3. Ground Distribution

Relief supplies are handed over directly to cut-off families by verified community volunteers living in the affected river belts.

4. Public Invoices & Verification

Dated purchase bills, manifests, and photo logs are published in the Updates section. No fabricated numbers or artificial counters.
