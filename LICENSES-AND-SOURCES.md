### Ethical Imagery Guidelines Followed
1. **Dignity & Respect:** No exploitative or sensationalized imagery of distress.
2. **Attribution & Transparency:** All visual depictions are labeled as illustrative logistics references unless accompanied by signed field logs.
3. **No False Beneficiary Claims:** Stock and illustrative subjects are never claimed to be named recipients of funds without their informed consent and verification.
